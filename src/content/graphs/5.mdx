## Cycles of length 5

The graph <Snark n={5} /> has eleven cycles of length 5.
Each block contains two, and the inner ring is the eleventh.
No other Goldberg snark has an inner ring of length 5.

## Longest cycle

A [_Hamiltonian cycle_](/#hamiltonian-cycles) is a cycle that visits every vertex.
Like every snark, <Snark n={5} /> has none.
Its longest cycles visit 39 of its 40 vertices.

## Connectivity

The _cyclic edge-connectivity_ of a graph is the smallest number of edges whose removal leaves two parts that each contain a cycle.
It is at least 4 for every snark, and [5 for <Snark n={5} />](/#properties).

## Number of snarks of this size

As of 2026, the number of snarks with 40 vertices, the size of <Snark n={5} />, is not known.
The largest size for which it is known is 38 vertices, with 1,051,945,967 snarks <Cite id="brinkmann2026" />.
