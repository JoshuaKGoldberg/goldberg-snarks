## Cycle of length 7

The inner ring of <Snark n={7} /> is a cycle of length 7.
It is the only such cycle in <Snark n={7} />, and no other Goldberg snark has one.

<Highlight
	blocks={7}
	kind="inner"
	label="The Goldberg snark G7, with the heptagon in its center emphasized."
>
	The inner ring of <Snark n={7} />.
</Highlight>

In every Goldberg snark, cycles that do not go around the ring of blocks have length 5, 6, or at least 8.
Of the cycles that do, the inner ring has length _k_, and all others have length at least 10.
A Goldberg snark therefore has a cycle of length 7 only if _k_ is 7.
This is shown in the proof of <Ref id="short-cycles" />.

## Other short cycles

Each block contains two cycles of length 5, which share the path _c_, _b_, _d_.
The remaining edges of the two cycles form a cycle of length 6.
There are no other cycles within a block.

<Highlight
	blocks={7}
	kind="block-cycles"
	label="The Goldberg snark G7, with a pentagon in its first block, through b, c, e, f, and d, and a hexagon in its third block, through c, e, f, d, h, and g, each emphasized."
>
	A cycle of length 5, at the top, and a cycle of length 6, at the right.
</Highlight>

A cycle that visits two or more blocks, other than the inner ring, has length at least 8.
Counted by computer, <Snark n={7} /> has 14 cycles of length 5, 7 of length 6, 1 of length 7, and 42 of length 8.
