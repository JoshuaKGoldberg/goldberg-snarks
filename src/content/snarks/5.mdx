## Longest cycle

Like every snark, <Snark n={5} /> has [no Hamiltonian cycle](/#hamiltonian).
Its longest cycles visit 39 of its 40 vertices.

## Connectivity

The definition of a snark requires that at least four edges must be removed to separate it into two parts that each contain a cycle.
For <Snark n={5} />, at least five must be removed.

## Number of snarks of this size

As of 2026, all snarks with up to 38 vertices are known <Cite id="brinkmann2026" />.
There are 1,051,945,967 snarks with exactly 38 vertices.
A cubic graph always has an even number of vertices, so the next size is 40.
The number of snarks with 40 vertices, the size of <Snark n={5} />, is not known.
