## Coverings

Since 15 is a multiple of 5, <Snark n={15} /> is a [covering graph](/g9#covering) of [<Snark n={5} />](/g5).
Each block of <Snark n={5} /> corresponds to three blocks of <Snark n={15} />.
It is the smallest Goldberg snark that is a covering graph of another Goldberg snark.

Since 15 is also a multiple of 3, <Snark n={15} /> is a covering graph of the Goldberg graph [<Snark n={3} />](/g3), which is not a snark.
Each block of <Snark n={3} /> corresponds to five blocks of <Snark n={15} />.

## Chinese remainder theorem

Number the blocks of <Snark n={15} /> from 0 to 14.
Block _i_ corresponds to the block of <Snark n={3} /> whose number is the remainder of _i_ divided by 3, and to the block of <Snark n={5} /> whose number is the remainder of _i_ divided by 5.
By the Chinese remainder theorem, each pair of remainders occurs exactly once, so the two blocks together identify one of the fifteen.
