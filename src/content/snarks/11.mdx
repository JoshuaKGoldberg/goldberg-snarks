## Eleven steps across

The _diameter_ of a graph is how many steps apart its two farthest vertices are.
The diameter of <Snark n={11} /> is eleven: the same as its number of blocks.
No other Goldberg snark's is.

<Highlight
	blocks={11}
	kind="farthest"
	label="The Goldberg snark G11, with a path of eleven edges from one side to the other emphasized."
>
	A shortest path between two vertices that are as far apart as any. It takes
	eleven steps.
</Highlight>

It's a race between two kinds of distance.
Getting into and out of the hub costs a few steps whatever the size, so small Goldberg snarks are further across than they have blocks: five blocks are six steps across, seven are eight, and nine are ten.
Once you're in the hub, though, each step passes a whole block, and you never have to pass more than half of them.
Every two blocks more add only one step.
At thirteen blocks, the diameter of twelve has fallen behind for good.

## The end of the record

<Snark n={11} /> can't be drawn on a flat page with fewer than 22 pairs of its
edges crossing. It's the largest of the Goldberg snarks for which that fewest
number is on record <Cite id="weisstein" />.
