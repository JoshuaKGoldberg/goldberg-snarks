## Diameter

The _distance_ between two vertices is the number of edges in a shortest path between them, and the _diameter_ of a graph is its largest distance.
The diameter of <Snark n={11} /> is 11.
It is the only Goldberg snark whose diameter equals its number of blocks.

	kind="farthest"
	label="The Goldberg snark G11, with a path of eleven edges from one side to the other emphasized. It goes in to the inner ring, around half of it, and back out."
>
	A shortest path between two vertices at distance 11.
</Highlight>

A shortest path between distant vertices follows either the outer or middle ring, or the inner ring.
The outer and middle rings take about two edges per block.
In <Snark n={5} />, <Snark n={7} />, and <Snark n={9} />, the vertices that are farthest apart are joined by such a path, and the diameter is one more than the number of blocks: 6, 8, and 10.

Every vertex is at most three edges from the inner ring, which takes one edge per block.
In <Snark n={11} />, a path by way of the inner ring has at most 3 + 5 + 3 = 11 edges.
For larger Goldberg snarks, every two additional blocks add one to the diameter.
The diameters of <Snark n={13} /> and <Snark n={15} /> are 12 and 13.

## Crossing number

The _crossing number_ of a graph is the smallest number of edge crossings in any drawing of it.
The crossing number of <Snark n={11} /> is 22 <Cite id="weisstein" />.
It is the largest Goldberg snark with a known crossing number.
