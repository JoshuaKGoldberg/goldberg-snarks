## Triangle

The inner ring of <Snark n={3} /> is a triangle.
The [usual definition of a snark](/#snark) does not allow cycles of fewer than five edges, so the smallest Goldberg snark is [<Snark n={5} />](/g5) <Cite id="mohar2006" />.

A triangle in a cubic graph can be shrunk to a single vertex without changing whether the graph has a 3-edge-coloring.
Some authors use a definition of a snark that allows triangles, and include <Snark n={3} /> among the Goldberg snarks <Cite id="campos2011" />.

## Relation to the Loupekine snarks

Shrinking the triangle of <Snark n={3} /> to a vertex leaves a graph with 22 vertices that is a snark.
It is one of the two smallest [Loupekine snarks](/#related-graphs), another family of snarks <Cite id="berman2017" />.

## Distances

The _diameter_ of a graph is the largest distance between two of its vertices, counted in edges.
Every vertex of <Snark n={3} /> has a vertex at distance 4 and none farther away, so the diameter is 4.
