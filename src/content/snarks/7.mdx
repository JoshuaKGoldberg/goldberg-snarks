## The only heptagon

Look through any Goldberg snark for cycles and you'll find some of five edges, some of six, and some of eight.
There are never any of seven, with one exception.

<Highlight
	blocks={7}
	kind="hub"
	label="The Goldberg snark G7, with the heptagon in its center emphasized."
>
	The hub of <Snark n={7} /> is a cycle of seven edges, and no Goldberg snark
	has another.
</Highlight>

It's because short cycles come in two kinds.
Some stay within a few neighboring blocks, and those look the same in every large enough ring.
They're the pentagons, two to a block, the hexagons, one between each pair of blocks, and cycles of at least eight.
The rest go all the way around, and the shortest way around is the hub.
So a Goldberg snark only has a cycle of seven edges if its hub is one.

## Out of the center

From seven blocks on, a hub is too wide to be a Goldberg snark's shortest cycle, or even tied for it.

<Snark n={7} /> is the first in the family whose only pentagons are its blocks'
own: fourteen of them, two for each block.
