## A symmetry for each card

A _symmetry_ of a graph rearranges its vertices such that any two that shared an edge still do.
With thirteen blocks there are 52 of them, as many as there are cards in a deck, and they're organized like one too: in thirteen ranks and four suits.

- The thirteen ranks are turns: each of the thirteen blocks can be moved to where any other was.
- The four suits are ways to do so: as it is, as if seen in a mirror, inside out, or both.

## Turning it inside out

The drawing shows the turns.
It hints at the mirror, though not perfectly, as its blocks are drawn at a slant.
Nothing about it suggests that it can be turned inside out.

In every block, swap _c_ with _d_, _e_ with _h_, and _f_ with _g_.
Each edge lands on an edge.
The outer ring has become the middle ring, the middle ring has become the outer one, and the graph is the same as it ever was.
That the one is drawn around the other is only a choice that the drawing made.

Every Goldberg snark can do this.
With _k_ blocks it has 4*k* symmetries, so only <Snark n={13} /> has a full deck.
Its 104 vertices even come to two per card.
