## Background

A _graph_ is a set of points, called vertices, joined by lines, called edges.
A graph is _cubic_ if three edges meet at every vertex.
A _cycle_ is a closed loop of edges that does not visit any vertex twice.

An _edge coloring_ gives each edge a color, with different colors for edges that meet.
Most cubic graphs have one with three colors, called a <span class="nowrap">_3-edge-coloring_</span>.
A _snark_ is a cubic graph that needs four colors, with some exceptions.

The _Goldberg snark_ <Snark n="k" /> is made of _k_ copies of a block of eight vertices, where _k_ is odd and at least 5 <Cite id="goldberg1981" />.
Links join each block to the next, in an <Term is="outer">outer ring</Term>, a <Term is="middle">middle ring</Term>, and an <Term is="inner">inner ring</Term>.
[The construction](/#construction) names the vertices of each block _a_ through _h_.
